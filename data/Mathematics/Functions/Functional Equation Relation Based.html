JEE Advanced: Functional Equations Cheat Sheet

1 The "Big Four" Cauchy Equations

These are the foundation. If the problem states the function is continuous or a polynomial, you can instantly assume these forms.

Functional Equation Result Function Type
\( f(x + y) = f(x) + f(y) \) \( f(x) = cx \) Linear
\( f(x + y) = f(x)f(y) \) \( f(x) = a^x \) Exponential
\( f(xy) = f(x) + f(y) \) \( f(x) = \log_a x \) Logarithm
\( f(xy) = f(x)f(y) \) \( f(x) = x^n \) Power

2 The "JEE Special" Results

These specific forms are frequently used as "traps" or "shortcuts" in Advanced papers.

A. The Inversive Identity

Equation: \( f(x) \cdot f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = f(x) + f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) \)

Result: \( f(x) = 1 \pm x^n \)

*Note: The sign (\(\pm\)) and power (\(n\)) are determined by the given range or specific values (like \( f(2) \)).*

B. Jensen’s Equation

Equation: \( f\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right) = \frac{f(x) + f(y)}{2} \)

Result: \( f(x) = ax + b \)

C. The Tangent Identity

Equation: \( f(x+y) = \frac{f(x) + f(y)}{1 - f(x)f(y)} \)

Result: \( f(x) = \tan(cx) \)

3 Tactical Strategies

  • 🎯
    The "0, 1, -1" Test: Always try plugging in \(x=0\), \(x=1\), or \(y=0\) first. It often collapses the equation and gives you \(f(0)\) or \(f(1)\), which are vital for finding constants.
  • ⚡
    The Derivative Move: If the function is differentiable, differentiate with respect to \(x\) while keeping \(y\) constant (partial differentiation). This often leads to a differential equation you can solve.
  • 📏
    Check the Range: Verify if the final function matches the given domain and range (e.g., \(1+x^n\) vs \(1-x^n\)).
