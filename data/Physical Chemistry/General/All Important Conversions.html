▲ TOP EXAM TRAPS — MEMORISE THESE
01 m³ vs dm³ vs cm³ — always convert to the same unit before substituting. 1 dm³ = 1 L = 10⁻³ m³. This trap appeared in 2020 & 2014 papers.
02 kPa vs Pa in W = −PΔV — convert P to Pa and V to m³ to get Joules, then ÷ 1000 for kJ. Don't mix units mid-calculation.
03 STP molar volume — 22.7 L (at 1 bar, new IUPAC) vs 22.4 L (at 1 atm, old). Check which standard the question uses.
04 R value — use 8.314 J/mol·K for thermodynamics, 0.0821 L·atm/mol·K for gas laws. Never mix them.
05 1 L·atm = 101.325 J — not 100 J. That's 1 L·bar = 100 J. Easy to mix up in electrochemistry.
